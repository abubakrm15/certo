Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mobile spyware and how Certo keeps you safe.

Can iPhones actually get infected with spyware?

Yes. While Apple maintains strict app sandboxing, commercial stalkerware and malicious configuration profiles can compromise iOS devices without triggering standard warnings.

How do I know if someone is tracking my phone?

Unusual battery drain, unexpected data usage spikes, strange background noises during calls, or unfamiliar keyboards are common signs of active surveillance.

Will running Certo slow down my device or drain battery?

No. Certo executes lightweight, on-demand and scheduled scans designed specifically to minimize resource consumption and maintain peak battery life.

Is my personal data sent to Certo servers?

No. We operate on a strict privacy-first principle. System audits and file hashes are processed locally on your phone without collecting personal photos, messages, or contacts.
